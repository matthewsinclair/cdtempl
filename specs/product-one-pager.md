# Product one-pager — specification

## What it is

The single page you leave behind after a conversation. It is read alone, usually
weeks later, usually by someone who was not in the room.

**One page is a constraint, not a target.** The discipline is what makes this asset
useful: it forces the choice of which one thing matters. A two-page one-pager is a
different document and a worse one.

## The standard shape

One page, explicitly paginated. Seven blocks:

| Block | Carries |
|---|---|
| Name and one-liner | What it is, for whom, in one sentence |
| The problem | One sentence, as the reader experiences it |
| What it does | Three points, maximum |
| Proof | A customer, a number, or a named comparison — one real thing |
| How it works | Three steps or one diagram slot |
| What it costs | A price, a range, or the honest reason there is not one yet |
| The action | One. Named specifically. |

Two notes. **Proof is the block that decides whether the page works** — a
one-pager with no proof is a brochure. And **the action is singular**: a page
offering three next steps gets none of them taken.

## Two versions, and they are not the same page

Customer and investor one-pagers share a layout and share almost no copy. The
customer page answers "what do I get"; the investor page answers "why does this
win". Producing one and using it for both is the most common failure with this
asset, and the brief must say which is being made.

## Formats produced

- `Product One-Pager.dc.html` — the source. Explicitly paginated: one page, fixed
  page box, designed to fill letter and A4 alike.
- `exports/product-one-pager.pdf` — the artefact that travels.

## Template form

**Form C — built artefact with placeholder content**, explicitly paginated because
the page count is the point. The layout is designed to fill the page box at both
letter and A4 without overlap and without viewport units.

## Definition of done

1. **It is one page.** At letter and at A4.
2. **The proof block contains something real**, or the page is not sent.
3. **There is exactly one action.**
4. **Every number is dated**, and illustrative ones carry the visible marker.
5. **The audience is declared** and the copy matches it.
6. **It reads without the conversation** that preceded it.
7. **Nothing below 12pt.**
8. **No blanks remain.**

## What the brief must carry

Beyond the universal header:

- Audience: customer or investor.
- The single action.
- The proof point, and whether it may be named publicly.
- Whether numbers are real or illustrative.
