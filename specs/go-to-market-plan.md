# Go-to-market plan — specification

## What it is

**How the first customers are reached, by whom, at what cost, and how anyone will know
whether it worked.** Not a channel list and not a marketing calendar — a plan with
resource attached and a stated definition of success.

The distinguishing feature of a real one is that it can fail. A plan naming six
channels with no budget, no owner and no target cannot be wrong, and so tells nobody
anything.

## The standard shape

| Part | Carries |
|---|---|
| The motion | How a customer actually gets bought — self-serve, sales-led, partner, hybrid |
| Segments | Who is approached first, and why them before the others |
| Channels | Each one with an owner, a cost, and an expected contribution |
| Sequence | What happens in what order, and what each stage has to prove |
| Measurement | The number that says it worked, and when it is read |
| Stop conditions | What result would mean abandoning a channel |

## A channel without an owner and a budget is a wish

**Every channel needs three things or it does not belong in the plan**: someone whose
job it is, money or time it costs, and what it is expected to produce. A list of
plausible channels is the commonest form of this document and the least useful, because
it survives contact with reality by being unfalsifiable.

**The motion decides everything downstream.** Self-serve and sales-led need different
pricing, different collateral and different site architecture, so naming the motion
late means rebuilding the things that assumed the other one.

## Definition of done

- The motion is named, and the plan is consistent with it throughout
- Every channel carries an owner, a cost and an expected contribution
- The first segment is justified against the others rather than merely listed
- Measurement names a number and a date, not a direction of travel
- **Stop conditions exist** — a plan that cannot be abandoned will not be

## What the brief must carry

The budget and who is doing the work. Without both, the plan will describe a venture
with a marketing team it does not have, and the first month will quietly become
whichever channel the founder already knew how to use.

Also what has already been tried and what happened. Prior attempts are the only real
evidence available, and a plan written without them tends to re-propose the thing that
did not work.
