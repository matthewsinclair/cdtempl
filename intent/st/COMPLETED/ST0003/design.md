# Design - ST0003: Post-release 0.1.0 clean-up

**There is no thread-level design, and that is deliberate rather than unfinished.**

ST0003 is a holding pen with structure, not a plan -- its work packages are independent by design, can be taken in any order, and most wait on a ruling rather than on effort. A design section here would have to invent a shape the thread does not have.

**Each work package carries its own design in its own `info.md`**, next to the evidence that motivated it. `WP/*/info.md` is the one home.

Rulings that outlast this thread go to `intent/whiteboard/cc/wip.md` `## Decisions`. Canon outranks both: `intent/docs/design-system-lifecycle.md`.

**This file replaced an unfilled template on 8 August.** The boilerplate read as a place where design lived, so a reader opened it and found prompts, and a writer might reasonably have put a WP's reasoning here instead of beside its evidence.
