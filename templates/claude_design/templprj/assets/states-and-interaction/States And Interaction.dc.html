{{ ventureName }} states

Every state a thing can be in. This is the asset that gets skipped: a component library shows components working, and states are what happen the other ninety percent of the time. A product whose empty states were never designed looks unfinished exactly when a new person arrives.

Because the kit is greyscale, every state here is already distinguishable without colour. That is not a limitation — it is the test. Apply a venture palette and this is the first thing that breaks.

Component states

A matrix, not an enumeration. Three representative components across nine states; the pattern generalises to the rest of the library. 28 components times 9 states is legible as a matrix and illegible as 252 examples.

Focus-visible is never removed, at any state combination — it is the row above in bold, and it is the one most often deleted because it is thought ugly. And read-only is not disabled: read-only text stays fully legible, because a person is meant to read it.

Motion

The neutral kit permits one thing: a 120ms opacity or position change. That is not a recommendation — it is a placeholder, so that no venture inherits a motion language nobody chose.
